Fernwaerts is a local-first app. Your phone does the day-to-day work: it records
locations, keeps a local copy of your history, and processes that data into the
movement segments, places, map, and timeline you see in the app. This includes
tasks such as detecting movement and reverse geocoding places.

The self-hosted server provides authentication and a durable copy of your data
so your history can persist and stay synchronized across multiple devices. It
is not the main processing engine for your location history.

Keeping everyday processing on the phone also leaves room for a future version
of Fernwaerts that works entirely locally, without a self-hosted server.
