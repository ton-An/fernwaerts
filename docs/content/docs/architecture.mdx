Fernwaerts is a local-first app. Your phone does the day-to-day work, and the
self-hosted server keeps a durable, synchronized copy of your data.

## What your phone does

Your phone is the main engine for your location history. It records your
locations and keeps a local copy of your history. From that data it builds the
movement segments, places, map, and timeline you see in the app, detecting
movement and reverse geocoding places along the way.

## What the server does

The self-hosted server provides authentication and a durable copy of your data,
so your history can persist and stay synchronized across multiple devices. It is
not the main processing engine for your location history.

Keeping everyday processing on the phone also leaves room for a future version
of Fernwaerts that works entirely locally, without a self-hosted server.
